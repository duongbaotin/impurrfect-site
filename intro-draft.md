We are not perfect. Our space is small, our furniture doesn't match, and our people are human. We built this with what we had. Our resident cats aren't flawless - they are survivors of the HCMC streets, some missing a leg, some with cloudy eyes, some with stories only the regulars know. But they are here: alive, thriving, and completely at home.

You don't have to be perfect here, either. Come as you are, dust yourself off, pet a cat, and have a fair pour.

We are not a high-end mixology lounge. We are a neighbourhood cat dive bar hidden in an alley. The drinks are proper, but the space is raw. We have no air conditioning, just the natural breeze and industrial fans. Smoking's fine, too, out in the alley.

If you are looking for perfection, you won't find it here. But if you need an honest drink, a quiet corner to decompress after work, and a cat who will accept you exactly as you are... welcome home.
