
I. COCKTAILS

1.
HOT BUTTERED WHISKEY
- Whiskey (Bourbon) 45ml
- Bơ lạt 1 tablespoon (~15ml)
- Simple Syrup 15ml
- Cinnamon Syrup 10ml
- Hot Water (not quite boiling) 75ml

process: pour one by one and stir to dissovle
garnish: whipped cream (preferably) / cinnamon stick
serve: rock glass or coupe

2.
PAINKILLER
- Dark Rum 45ml
- Pineapple Juice 45ml
- Orange Juice 30ml
- Coconut Milk / Cream of Coconut 30ml

process: shake
garnish: Orange wheel + grated nutmeg
serve: tiki glass

3.
BLUE COCONUT MARGARITA
- Tequila 45ml
- Blue Curacao 15ml
- Lime Juice 15ml
- Cream of Coconut 10ml

process: shake
garnish: rim glas with salt/sugar + lime wheel
serve: coupe or rock glass

4.
LAVA LADY
- Gin 45ml
- Lemon Juice 22.5ml

- Chilli Grenadine 15ml

garnish: no
serve: coupe glass

5.
WATERMELON MARTINI

II. MOCKTAILS
1.
COCONUT MINT COOLER
- Coconut Water 150ml
- Mint Leaves 
- Lime Juice 10ml
- Simple Syrup 10ml

process: 
- muddle mint leaves + simple syrup
- add lime juice & coconut water
- shake
garnish: Mint leaves + Lime wheel
glassware: Highball
taste profile: cool, refreshing, slightly tangy

2.
COCONUT PINEAPPLE FIZZ
- Coconut Water 120ml
- Pineapple Juice 60ml
- Lime Juice 10ml
- Top with Soda water

process:
- Mix Coconut water, pineapple & lime juice
- Pour over ice
- Top with soda
garnish: Pineapple slice + cherry (optional)
glassware: Collins
taste profile: Sweet, Fizzy, Tropical

3.
COCONUT WATERMELON SPLASH
- Coconut Water 100ml
- Watermelon Juice 80ml
- Lime Juice 10ml
- Honey Syrup 5ml

process:
- combine all ingredients
- shake well
- serve over ice
garnish: Watermelon wedge + Mint
glassware: Highball
taste profile: Light, Juicy, Refreshing

4.
COCONUT STRAWBERRY REFRESHER
- Coconut Water 120ml
- Strawberry Syrup 20ml
- Strawberries 3
- Lime Juice 10ml
- Simple Syrup 10ml

process:
- muddle berries + syrups
- add lime juice + coconut water
- shake + strain over ice
garnish: strawberries
glassware: Collins
taste profile: Light, Juicy, Refreshing

5.
STRAWBERRY MEETS ORANGE
- Strawberry Syrup 15ml
- Orange Juice 45ml
- Soda 100ml

process:
- add strawberry syrup + 50ml soda, stir
- add ice
- top with orange juice
garnish: lemon wheel / orange wheel
glassware: Collins / Tiki
