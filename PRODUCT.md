# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bar patrons in their mid-20s to 40s, local to Vietnam or travelers, looking for the menu, hours, location, and vibe of a hidden speakeasy. They're curious, adventurous with their drink choices, and appreciate craft over hype. Some are planning a visit, others are already at the bar browsing the menu on their phone.

## Product Purpose

A digital storefront for The Impurrfect Speakeasy. It exists to communicate the bar's wabi-sabi philosophy, showcase the cocktail menu, and give patrons everything they need to find and visit the bar. Success means the site feels like an extension of the bar itself — dark, warm, intimate, slightly unexpected — not just a digital brochure.

## Brand Personality

Wabi-sabi, warm, quietly confident. Three words: **unpolished, intentional, warm.** The bar embraces imperfection but nothing about it is accidental. The voice is direct, not flowery. The aesthetic is speakeasy intimacy without the velvet-rope attitude.

## Anti-references

- Generic cocktail bar templates. No stock photo of a muddled drink, no "craft cocktail" cliché typography, no Instagram-gradient sunsets.
- Over-polished corporate hospitality sites. No sterile white backgrounds, no professional food photography that looks like a menu at a chain restaurant.
- Pretentious luxury. No gold foil textures, no "handcrafted" in script fonts, no velvet rope energy.

## Design Principles

1. **Dark warmth, not dark cool.** The site lives at night. Backgrounds are warm blacks and deep charcoals, not navy or slate. Light is gold and cream, not white or blue.
2. **Unpolished intentionality.** Nothing should look templated or auto-generated. Spacing can be unexpected. Layouts can break the grid. But everything has a reason.
3. **Substance over flash.** The cocktails are the stars. Decorative elements (glass SVGs, brand word) earn their place. If it doesn't help someone decide what to drink or how to find the bar, it probably shouldn't be there.
4. **Typography as atmosphere.** The type system does the heavy lifting for mood. Playfair Display for elegance, monospace-leaning body for grit. No decorative fonts, no script.
5. **Low friction, high character.** Get the information to the user fast. Menu loads in one click. Hours and address are always one scroll away. The character comes through in the details, not in loading delays or scroll-jacking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Body text contrast at least 4.5:1 against background. Interactive elements have visible focus states. Reduced motion respected. The site should work for someone using a screen reader to find the bar's address and hours.
